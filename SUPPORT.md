# Support

RhythmCoach is currently distributed as a public beta.

## Before reporting an issue

Please include:

- Browser and operating system.
- Whether RhythmCoach was opened in a normal tab or installed as a PWA.
- Training mode used.
- Whether microphone permission was granted.
- Clear steps to reproduce the problem.

Do not attach private scripts or sensitive recordings unless you intentionally choose to share them.

## Reporting bugs and suggestions

Use the repository issue tracker:

https://github.com/liuh886/RhythmCoach/issues

For microphone, recording, or PWA problems, note whether the behavior also occurs after refreshing the page and granting permission again.
